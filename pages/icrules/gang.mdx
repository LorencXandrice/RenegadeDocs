# §3 😎 Gang Rules 😎

1. Groups (or Gangs or Families) are limited to 15 people max.

2. Command of any government entity cannot be on the roster of a gang/family/motorcycle club.\
a. All government entities need SOPs regarding corruption. 

3. On day-to-day activities as well as group activities, you must wear a clothing item (e.g., group bandana, group color, cuts, etc.) that indicates you are part of that group.\
a. Exception - In a robbery scene, it is optional to wear your group clothing.\
b. To establish your group, your group must be active in Renegade to the extent that many people can associate your specified color with your group.\
c. Once you have a few people in your group and your group can be recognized by many people in the city, you may express your interest to become an **official group** via via [`📋Applications`](https://discord.com/channels/1083964697532973157/1083965258240110594).\
d. You may fight people for your group bandana, group color, cuts, etc., but be reasonable with other players. (e.g., Don't shoot someone not in your group over a something as simple as the color of underglow/tire smokes.)

4. When in a war, you must state what is considered your group's clothing (i.e., a yellow face bandana) for the other group to look for.

5. Gang wars cannot last forever. A ending must be determined from both sides.\
a. If a war has lasted too long or there is no ending, then staff can intervene with the group leaders.

6. If you create a <font color="#5661F5"> **Discord** </font> for your group, a member of staff must be in the Discord with full admin permissions or able to see all channels with no restrictions.
