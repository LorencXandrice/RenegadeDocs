# §6 🥳 Your First Day on Renegade 🥳

You have successfully created your character -- Congrats! We hope you're here for a good time and a long time.

Your character will be exiting the Alta Penthouse. We recommend doing the following:

#### **Familiarize Yourself with Your Inventory**
- *Press* **`Tab`** to open your inventory to see your starting items.

#### **Setup Your Phone**

- *Press* **`M`** to pull up your phone from your pockets and begin the setup process.
    - *Click* on the **`Apps`** icon and we recommend downloading all the apps.
    - Make a **Y** account and start interacting with other people.
        - Y is the main method our community meets and interacts with one another.
    - Request a rideshare via the **`RideMe`** app.

- *Press* **`ESC`** to open your map and familiarize yourself with all the points of interest.
    - You will be given a **`Starter Apartment`**. Open your map to find it.
    - Visit a community member food business for food and drinks with higher caloric value than the offerings from your local 24/7 store.

- Within you **`F1`** radial menu, you can quickly the quickest route to the nearest Barber Shop, Clothing Shop, Stores (24/7 Grocery Stores), Bennys, Ammunation, and Gas Station.

    - Speak to Brenda and **`Claim Your Daily Reward`**!
